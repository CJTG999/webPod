Features

Everything a pocket player needs

A nostalgic shell with modern guts: Web Audio DSP, IndexedDB storage, and live lyric sync.

The Toolkit

Four pages, one ecosystem

Everything shares the same browser storage, so your songs and captions follow you between tools.

How it works

From file to singalong in four steps

  1. 1

    Drop your music

    Upload MP3, WAV, or OGG files. Titles and artists are read from ID3 tags automatically.

  2. 2

    Get lyrics

    Pull synced lyrics from LRCLIB, or attach / paste your own .SRT and .LRC files.

  3. 3

    Tune it

    Dial in the EQ, bass boost, playback speed, and nudge caption timing with offset and rate sliders.

  4. 4

    Back it up

    Export your whole library, lyrics and offsets included, as a single ZIP and restore it anywhere.
